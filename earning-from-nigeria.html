Compare the possibilities

Each route makes a different promise.

What is being paid for?
RouteThe commercial exchange
ServicesA defined piece of work and your judgment.
Digital productsA resource that helps complete a task.
TeachingExplanation, practice and appropriate feedback.
SubscriptionsContinuing access, research or participation.
LicensingPermission for an agreed use of existing work.
AdvertisingAn agreed opportunity to present a commercial message.
SponsorshipA defined association and agreed project benefits.
Affiliate referralsThe programme rewards qualifying referrals under its terms.
SpeakingA prepared contribution to an event or discussion.

Choose a route you can deliver responsibly. Developing several possibilities does not require launching them all at once.
