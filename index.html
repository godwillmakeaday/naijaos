§ 04

Source discipline

naijaOS works from public information, official releases, and reported indicators. Every figure is dated and attributed to the release it came from. Where a number is contested or unverified, the page says so rather than smoothing it over.

This is intelligence, not advice. naijaOS is not legal, financial, tax, or investment advice. Conditions change. Verify against current official sources before acting. Diagnosis is kept visibly separate from recommendation on every page, and corrections are published rather than quietly edited.
