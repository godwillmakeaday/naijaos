Websites, AI, learning and an international presence

I am exploring how someone working from Nigeria can turn knowledge, professional experience and ideas into useful work that reaches people elsewhere. This series follows the process: platforms, attempts, learning, publication and the commercial possibilities that may follow.

Article 01 / Personal reflection

Building from Nigeria for the World

I live in Nigeria, but I do not believe the value of my work should end at Nigeria’s borders.

That conviction is behind my growing interest in websites, artificial intelligence, programming and the platforms through which people publish and build. I want to understand how someone working from here can create something useful enough for people elsewhere to read, use, trust and eventually pay for.

How does a person establish a presence through which their value can travel?

An idea can remain in a notebook for years. A skill can remain known only to friends. Someone may possess a useful perspective but have no dependable way for others to discover it. I have become interested in the distance between possessing something valuable and making it available.

Give the work somewhere to live

A website allows me to organise what I am building. An article can sit beside the project it explains. A guide can connect to a worksheet. Different pieces can accumulate until a visitor begins to understand the thinking behind them.

I practise law, think about institutions and ask questions about enterprise and opportunity. A passing conversation rarely gives those interests enough room. A website allows me to develop their connections over time.

Someone can encounter an essay and examine a project without first knowing me personally. That is the international presence I want: a growing body of work people can inspect.

The tools become practical

Once I started building, apparently distant questions became immediate. Where should the website live? How do I connect a domain? Why does a page behave differently on a phone? How do I correct something after publication?

Those questions have led me into hosting platforms, website editors, AI and programming. Each tool earns its place through the task it helps me accomplish.

AI has made me willing to attempt projects that once seemed harder to approach. I can discuss an idea, inspect a first version and work through difficulties. I still need to assess the result and take responsibility for what I publish.

Reach is an opening

Being online does not guarantee an audience. An audience does not guarantee income. People need a reason to return, and buyers need a reason to trust the work.

My ambition is to create value that can justify a fair price internationally while I work from Nigeria. That requires useful work, clear communication and dependable delivery. The website gives that ambition somewhere to begin.

This series follows my attempts: the choices, costs, mistakes and discoveries. Where an experiment remains planned, I will identify it as such. Where the record does not establish a result, I will leave the question open.

I am building from here. I want the work to travel.

A first step of your own

Write one sentence identifying whom you want to help and what they should be able to do after encountering your work. Then choose one useful thing you can publish or demonstrate. Keep that sentence beside the project as you build.

Article 02 / Personal account

Why I Use Hostinger: My Experience Building from Nigeria

Subjective experience, not a hosting benchmark. No current prices or server location are asserted.

When I choose a platform for a website, I think about what it allows me to accomplish. Can I afford to begin? Can I understand enough to use it? Can I publish my work and continue improving it?

These questions help explain why I use Hostinger. My website, paulmagaji.com, uses WordPress on Hostinger. My experience has felt affordable, responsive in everyday use and useful across several tasks involved in establishing an online presence.

Those are personal assessments. They need the context of what I am trying to do.

Affordability includes continuing

A website can take time to find its audience. During that period, I need room to publish, experiment and discover what readers find valuable. Costs affect how much room I have.

The practical question is whether I can sustain the arrangement. An introductory amount alone does not answer that. I need to examine the billing period, renewal terms and any additional services I actually require.

For my purposes, Hostinger has felt accessible enough to support the process. Another person needs to make their own calculation using the terms available when they purchase. I have not supplied a current price comparison here.

Responsiveness and useful capabilities

Building involves many small actions: open a page, change text, check the result and adjust it again. I appreciate a setup that feels responsive while I work.

That experience is different from a measured performance comparison. I have not established that Hostinger is faster than competing providers or that another website would perform like mine.

I also value having capabilities within reach that support publishing and maintenance. But access to many features is not the same as using them well. I want to become more precise about which capabilities help me regularly and which I merely have available.

Identify the source of a difficulty

My setup involves hosting, WordPress, a theme, plugins and the device I use to edit. A problem in one layer can feel like a failure of the entire arrangement.

I have encountered mobile-editing frustrations. Those deserve an account, but an awkward editor does not by itself establish that the hosting caused the difficulty. Separating the layers helps me troubleshoot and write fairly.

Similarly, using a service from Nigeria, or encountering a Nigerian-facing version of its website, does not establish that its servers are in Nigeria. I have not verified server location for this article.

A recommendation with boundaries

My position is straightforward: Hostinger has been useful in helping me develop an online presence from Nigeria. I cannot turn that experience into a universal verdict about the best provider.

Before choosing, define the website you need, examine the full payment commitment and ask what maintaining it will involve. My account gives you one experience to consider alongside those requirements.

Your comparison note

List your essential features, initial commitment, renewal commitment and likely extras for each option. Date the note. A relevant comparison of two actual plans is more useful than a general contest between brand names.

Article 03 / Historical account

When Connecting a Domain Consumes Your Day: My Truehost Experience

Provider corrected from Bluehost to Truehost in the recovered conversation record. The record does not confirm a successful resolution or establish the cause.

I wanted people to visit LandDesk through landdesk.ng. The project was on Vercel; the domain needed to connect to it. I expected a finishing task. Instead, it became a troubleshooting exercise that absorbed much of my day.

I changed settings, sought assistance and checked again. At one point, the system displayed “Command completed successfully.” Yet I still lacked confirmation that visitors would reach the intended website.

The experience taught me to ask what an apparent success actually establishes.

Correct the provider first

During the earlier discussion, I referred to Bluehost. The recovered troubleshooting record identified the registrar interface as Truehost. This article therefore concerns that Truehost-related interaction, rather than a verified Bluehost experience.

The distinction matters. A useful account needs to identify the service accurately. It also reveals a learning difficulty: the registrar, website host and DNS service can occupy different places in the same project. Calling all of them “the host” obscures where a problem belongs.

What the record establishes

The troubleshooting explored nameserver settings and DNS records. Attempts included Vercel nameservers and an arrangement involving default Cloudoon nameservers and an A record. There was also a DNS-zone creation step.

These are historical attempts, not instructions for another website. I have deliberately omitted an old IP address because copying an unverified historical setting would not establish a correct configuration today.

At one stage, the browser displayed DNS_PROBE_FINISHED_NXDOMAIN. I reported that nameserver settings appeared to revert. Support indicated that updates had been applied, but the result I needed remained unclear.

The record does not establish why this happened or confirm a successful resolution of that session.

The cost of uncertain progress

I considered transferring the domain because the difficulty was consuming time and I found the support experience frustrating. I wanted a manageable way forward.

The problem was increasingly one of knowing the current state. Which configuration was active? Had the requested change persisted? Was I examining the same arrangement that support was describing?

An assurance and an observed result seemed difficult to reconcile. More changes would not necessarily resolve that uncertainty.

What I would preserve next time

I would keep a clear record of the starting configuration, each intervention and the observation afterwards. That would make the discussion easier to follow and help avoid confusing a requested change with a confirmed one.

AI helped me discuss unfamiliar terms and possible explanations. Its usefulness still depended on identifying the provider correctly and checking the actual system.

The outcome I wanted was a visitor opening landdesk.ng and reaching LandDesk. Everything else was a step towards it.

A question for your own investigation

When a service reports success, write down exactly what it has confirmed. Then identify the separate observation that would establish your intended outcome. Keep both in the record.

Article 04 / Retrospective method

How I Approached a Domain Connection Problem—and What I Would Do Differently

A companion to an unresolved investigation, not a verified fix or a current DNS configuration guide.

When landdesk.ng would not connect as intended to my project on Vercel, I wanted a solution quickly. The more uncertain the result became, the more tempting it was to try another change.

Looking back, I would spend more time establishing the state of the system before the next intervention.

This is a companion to my Truehost account. The recovered record does not establish a completed fix. It offers lessons about organising an investigation while learning.

Describe the failure precisely

“The website is not working” leaves several possibilities open. Does the project fail everywhere? Does its deployment address open while the custom domain fails? Do different versions of the address behave differently?

I would begin by recording the address checked, the exact result and the time. During my investigation, the browser displayed DNS_PROBE_FINISHED_NXDOMAIN. Preserving that message gave the conversation something concrete to examine.

I would also record whether the separate deployment address worked. That helps identify which part of the journey needs attention.

Map responsibility

The original conversation involved confusion between Bluehost and Truehost. Correcting the provider was necessary before attributing the difficulty.

I would explicitly identify the registrar, deployment service and active DNS manager. Then I would establish which settings each controlled.

That map would help me ask for assistance in the right place. It would also make it easier to recognise when a proposed change belonged to a different configuration.

Give each intervention a purpose

Before making a change, I would record the current settings and answer three questions:

  • What observation makes this change relevant?
  • What result would support the explanation behind it?
  • What would I investigate if the result stayed the same?

Afterwards, I would distinguish what the interface accepted from what an independent observation confirmed. If several changes happened before another check, the result would be harder to interpret.

Make support requests answerable

A useful request identifies the goal, domain, observed failure and relevant changes already attempted. It then asks a precise question about the current state.

I would preserve the replies alongside my observations. If an assurance and the visible result differed, I could point to the discrepancy rather than beginning the explanation again.

Screenshots may help, but they should exclude passwords, verification codes and private account information.

Define completion

For my purpose, completion required the intended domain to reach the intended project. I also wanted enough understanding to maintain the arrangement.

Until that was verified, I should describe an investigation or attempted fix. A successful command message alone would not settle the larger question.

A reusable troubleshooting record

For your next technical problem, keep these fields together: goal; exact failure; current setup; proposed explanation; change attempted; observed result; unresolved question. This records a method without pretending that one historical configuration is a universal solution.

Article 05 / Working method

How I Use AI to Turn Ideas into Websites

Many of my website projects begin as questions. How can people understand the systems shaping their lives? How can someone examine a property decision more carefully? How can financial knowledge become easier to apply?

A question develops into an idea. Then I imagine somewhere a person could explore it and take a practical step.

AI has made it easier to move towards a working version. The process also reveals how much clarification the idea still needs.

Begin with the visitor’s task

Before discussing colours, I try to explain what the website should help someone accomplish. For NaijaOS, the purpose has developed towards understanding Nigeria and how people can understand, create and govern new possibilities.

That direction helps me decide whether an essay, diagram or tool belongs. Without a clear purpose, attractive pages can accumulate without a compelling reason to use them.

I want to translate the ambition into a task a visitor can recognise. What should they understand or be able to do after using this page?

Use conversation to develop the structure

I do not always begin with a complete specification. Sometimes I explain an observation, move between examples and correct wording that changes the meaning.

Conversation helps make the project clearer. But a persuasive response can drift away from my intention. I have to notice that and redirect it.

When developing a new NaijaOS section, preserving the existing site mattered. The new idea needed a place within the work already there. Explaining that condition changed the design task.

Build something I can question

A first version gives me evidence. A heading that sounded strong may feel vague on a page. A diagram may be difficult to read on a phone. A button may fail to explain its destination.

I can then give more precise feedback. Instead of requesting an undefined improvement, I can identify what a visitor would struggle to understand.

Another version is useful when it addresses an observed difficulty. Repeated changes of appearance alone do not establish progress.

Bring professional judgment

My legal background makes me attentive to claims, evidence and authority. Those concerns belong in design.

A property observation should not acquire the appearance of professional verification because its interface looks polished. A financial page should make its commercial relationships understandable.

AI can help express distinctions; I need to ensure they remain meaningful throughout the experience.

Examine the result

The questions become practical: do links open, controls work, downloads produce the intended files and pages remain readable on a narrow screen?

I increasingly want an account of what has been implemented, what has been checked and what remains uncertain. That helps me decide the next action.

My aim is a useful place, with clear writing, dependable tasks and a setup I can maintain.

Try a bounded brief

Describe one audience, one task, the information needed and the outcome expected. State which existing work must be preserved. Use that brief to assess the first version before adding more features.

Article 06 / Personal reflection

Building with ChatGPT: What I Do and What AI Does

When I say I use ChatGPT to build websites, the description can hide the most interesting part: how the work is divided.

I bring a purpose and its circumstances. Through conversation, that purpose develops into a structure and something I can inspect. Sometimes I need help organising my thinking; sometimes technical assistance. At other times, I need to explain why a proposed direction misses the point.

Understanding those moments helps me use the tool deliberately.

I bring context

A general request for a property website leaves many possibilities open. A request to organise property observations while making clear that they do not establish ownership sets a more precise task.

Likewise, my interest in Nigerian institutions, power and opportunity gives NaijaOS a particular direction. ChatGPT can develop an idea, but the circumstances help establish why it is worth developing.

I also decide what belongs. When a new section must respect an existing design, I need to make that condition explicit.

AI helps make the work inspectable

A long conversation can become a proposed set of pages, an article sequence or a description of a tool’s behaviour. Seeing the structure lets me identify what I meant and where the interpretation differs.

The fluency of the response can create an early impression of agreement. I therefore need to examine assumptions, scope and emphasis before allowing the proposal to become the foundation for more work.

My corrections are part of building.

Technical help needs actual information

I can ask about code, deployment or errors in ordinary language. That makes unfamiliar work easier to approach.

But a reasonable explanation based on an assumed setup may address the wrong problem. Exact errors, relevant pages, current configuration and earlier changes give the conversation a firmer starting point.

When I understand a little more of the technology, I can also follow the answer more closely and recognise what needs clarification.

I remain responsible for the claims

While preparing this series, a recovered record identified Truehost where I had referred to Bluehost. It also did not establish a successful resolution. Those details changed the article.

A readable account must still be accurate. I need to inspect sources, distinguish facts from possibilities and avoid presenting an unfinished investigation as a completed fix.

The same responsibility applies to what I publish through my projects.

Progress needs a clear description

A page written, a feature implemented, a check passed and a public deployment are different kinds of progress. I want to know which has occurred.

Before deciding the next action, I ask: what changed, what evidence supports it and what remains unverified?

ChatGPT helps me attempt more. My purpose is also to become a better director and maintainer of the work.

A useful instruction to reuse

Ask for a result together with its assumptions, changes made, checks performed and unresolved questions. Then examine the actual work. A confident account of completion needs evidence you can understand.

Article 07 / Planned evaluation

What I Want to Find Out by Building with Lovable

No completed Lovable test is documented here. This is an evaluation plan, not a product review or a claim about current capabilities.

As I explore ways to turn ideas into websites, I keep asking how much of the process I can manage myself and where I will need help.

That question shapes my interest in Lovable. This article sets out a planned evaluation. It does not report a completed hands-on test, establish current product capabilities or recommend a paid plan.

I want to examine the platform through a real project: a purpose, an attempt, revisions and an observable result.

Choose a small experiment

An idea may be less complete than it first appears. I may know the subject without knowing how visitors should move through it.

For an initial experiment, I would choose one audience, one problem and an identifiable outcome. A small project would make it easier to connect instructions with results. A large platform might hide too many unanswered questions.

Before beginning, I would define what completion meant for that task.

Assess purpose before appearance

An attractive version would be encouraging. I would still ask whether someone could accomplish the intended task.

For a worksheet, would the questions be understandable? For an article collection, could readers find a useful starting point? For a service page, would visitors understand the offer and the next step?

If the result missed something essential, I would examine both my instructions and the interpretation.

Record the revisions

The first version begins the experiment. I would then adjust wording, change the arrangement and add a requirement I had overlooked.

Could I describe the problem precisely? Did the revision address it? Did another part change unexpectedly?

A record would distinguish the initial output from the version achieved after repeated intervention. That matters when assessing claims about building speed.

Examine continuing control and cost

A public project needs updates and repairs. I would investigate how changes are made, which services the project depends on and what options exist for retaining access to the work.

Those questions require checking current features and terms during the experiment. I should not assume the answers from an early impression.

I would also record the cost of revisions, publication and any additional services. An account of what I paid should identify what that amount did and did not cover.

Test the visitor’s task

I would use an actual phone to examine reading, navigation and any forms. If the project included a task, I would try to complete it rather than judge screenshots alone.

The findings might reveal greater independence or a need for specialist assistance. Either would be useful.

The evaluation record

My planned record contains: project brief; dated product terms checked; initial instructions; first result; revisions; costs; task checks; remaining limitations. A later experience article should follow that evidence, including a decision to stop if the tool does not suit the task.

Article 08 / Learning direction

Why I Am Learning React While Building with AI

A learning goal, not a claim of React proficiency or a technical tutorial.

The more I use AI to help build websites, the more interested I become in understanding the work underneath.

If I can describe a page and receive help producing it, why spend time learning the technology? Because I want to direct a project, assess changes and maintain what I publish.

React has become one of the subjects I want to learn for that purpose. I am approaching it with projects to develop and questions to answer.

Building exposes the gaps

I can explain what I want visitors to see and do. Implementation introduces questions I cannot always answer: what is a component, where does information come from and why does one adjustment affect several pages?

Isolated definitions help, but I want to recognise the relationships within an actual project. Then I can follow the consequences of a proposed change.

That understanding should make technical decisions less mysterious.

Give better instructions

Suppose someone is completing a worksheet. What happens after they enter information? Should the entries remain when they move elsewhere? What should happen when a field is blank?

These begin as questions about the user. Technical learning helps me describe the expected behaviour more precisely and assess whether the implementation matches it.

I also want to understand repeated elements. If navigation or article cards appear across several pages, where should a shared improvement happen? How can a necessary difference fit without making maintenance confusing?

Improve judgment without overstating competence

Quickly produced code can be tempting to judge by its visible result. A convincing page may still behave poorly under different conditions.

A little React knowledge will not make me capable of finding every weakness. It can help me ask more informed questions and recognise when I need specialist assistance.

Can I explain what this part does? Do I understand the reason for the change? What check would show that the behaviour is correct?

Keep the learning goal manageable

I could collect courses for a long time without becoming more capable. I want to connect study with a particular need.

A repeated page element gives me a reason to study organisation. An interactive worksheet gives me questions about inputs and changing information. If the difficulty comes from JavaScript basics, I need to address that gap rather than assume another framework lesson will settle it.

The project gives the lesson somewhere to go.

Learn to maintain

Launching creates continuing work. I want to locate relevant material, describe a failure and understand the scope of a correction. Recognising the limit of my understanding is part of that capability.

My immediate goal is to open a project, recognise its main parts and explain a small feature. From there, I can develop further.

A practical learning check

Choose a small, separate exercise. Explain its expected behaviour, make one limited change and inspect the result. Record why it behaved as it did. Keep learning experiments apart from changes to a public website.

Article 09 / Learning approach

Learning Programming with a Project in Mind

When I think about learning programming, I think about things I want to build: clear publications, organised information and tools that help someone work through a problem.

Those ambitions give study a direction. Programming is a large field; without a purpose, I could move between courses while remaining uncertain about what I can do.

A project gives the learning somewhere to go.

Reduce the immediate ambition

A complete platform may involve accounts, payments, databases and several kinds of users. Each introduces questions that a beginner can struggle to separate.

For an exercise, I would choose one useful task. An article page can provide questions about structure and presentation. A small calculator gives a reason to examine inputs and calculations.

The larger ambition can remain. The immediate exercise needs a boundary and a clear definition of completion.

Let questions guide the next lesson

When a page is poorly arranged, I have a reason to investigate structure and styling. When a button must change something, I have a reason to understand behaviour.

The concept now belongs to work I care about. After studying it, I can return and see whether I understand enough to apply it.

Sometimes the difficulty reveals a missing prerequisite. That is useful information about where to begin again.

Give a course a defined job

Online courses can provide a sequence that scattered explanations lack. Before choosing one, I would ask what it should enable me to understand or do, what knowledge it assumes and what practice it requires.

Watching a lesson can create a feeling of progress. Attempting the task myself reveals more.

I want to allow time for that attempt, including the frustration of discovering which parts I have not understood.

Use AI to deepen practice

I can ask for a smaller example or an explanation connected to my project. But if AI immediately completes every exercise, I may never discover what I can do.

I want to attempt the task, describe where I became stuck and investigate the explanation. Then I can try again with less assistance.

A deliberate variation is useful: can I add an item, change a behaviour or explain the expected result before editing?

Preserve the lesson

When I make progress, I want to record what I expected, what happened and what changed my understanding. A short note helps me recover the lesson later.

It can also become an honest article. Solving one exercise does not establish mastery, but it can establish something specific I learned.

I want to measure progress through growing capability: explaining a part, making a limited change and describing a difficulty precisely enough to seek assistance.

A manageable study session

Choose one question arising from the project. Study the relevant concept, attempt a small exercise away from the public site and explain the result in your own words. Finish by recording the next unanswered question. That gives the following session a starting point.

Article 10 / Publishing lessons

From an AI Preview to a Live Website: What I Had to Learn

Seeing an idea become a page is exciting. Headings, sections and controls give a visible form to something that existed only in conversation.

Then another stage begins. Where does the website live? How does the domain reach it? Do its links work? What happens on a phone? How will tomorrow’s correction reach the public version?

My projects have taught me to distinguish a convincing preview from a website ready for visitors.

Examine it without the conversation

I know what each section is supposed to mean. A new visitor does not.

I need to ask whether someone can understand where they are, find the next step and receive what the page promises. The preview gives me something to assess; it does not establish every part of that experience.

I also need to know which version I am examining. A working copy, preview and public website can represent different states of the project.

Understand the publishing arrangement

My work includes WordPress on Hostinger and projects involving GitHub and Vercel. Each arrangement has its own way of storing changes and making them public.

When I request an improvement, I want to know where the change is happening and what would publish it. I also want a way to recover earlier work if necessary.

My landdesk.ng investigation made the domain connection especially tangible. A saved setting did not settle whether the intended address reached the intended project.

Review the content separately

Technical progress can distract from the words. Are claims supported? Do references open? Has placeholder material remained? Does an unfinished feature appear available?

I have encountered references returning errors. A link’s presence did not establish that it led to useful evidence.

The same attention belongs to contact details, navigation and calls to action. I need to follow the path I ask readers to follow.

Check promises through tasks

A download promises a file. A worksheet promises a process someone can complete. A print option promises a usable result.

Each needs examination at the level of the task: which file arrived, could it open, and did the printed version preserve the necessary information?

Mobile use deserves direct attention too. Can I read comfortably, use the controls and understand what happens after an action? A narrow-screen screenshot alone cannot answer every question.

Decide deliberately

A passed check establishes something specific. The remaining uncertainty still needs judgment, with the scope of review following what the website asks people to do.

Publication begins continuing care: corrections, changing references and unexpected visitor difficulties. I want a setup I can understand and maintain.

My release note

For the next publication, I would record the version, intended audience, main tasks checked, content reviewed and unresolved limitations. I would identify how to correct or withdraw the change. That makes “live” a more meaningful statement than a page appearing somewhere online.

Publishing makes work available. Finding an audience requires understanding whose attention it deserves and how it could become useful in their lives.

That is the next part of the work I want to develop. This article describes my approach, rather than a claim of an established large following.

Give someone a reason to visit

A visitor has no obligation to explore my interests. They may arrive with a question or decision. The page needs to explain why it concerns them.

For NaijaOS, the ambition includes understanding Nigeria and creating possibilities. An individual article needs a more immediate entry: a question about institutions, a small enterprise or building a website with AI.

A useful title makes the subject recognisable. The opening needs to deliver on that invitation.

Begin with a reader I can understand

“The world” is an ambition, but it gives little guidance about the next paragraph.

For this series, I can imagine a Nigerian professional with useful knowledge and limited publishing experience. They may wonder what they can build, how much technology to learn and which difficulties to expect.

I can address those questions through my attempts. Readers elsewhere may recognise them too. Clear circumstances help a local experience travel.

Make the work inspectable

An account of building becomes stronger when readers can examine the project. Troubleshooting becomes more useful when it distinguishes attempted solutions from confirmed outcomes.

The reader then has a basis for judgment beyond my enthusiasm.

I also need to help one visit lead somewhere relevant. Someone reading about programming may need the React article; someone preparing to publish may need the account of moving from preview to public website.

A collection gains value when its parts answer related questions.

Share with context

When sharing a link, I want to explain the question or lesson. A concrete observation gives someone something to consider before opening the article.

Listen for usefulness

A reader’s question may expose an incomplete explanation. A broken-link report may reveal a maintenance problem. An account of applying a framework may show practical value.

Traffic can help identify interest, but numbers need interpretation. I want to understand why someone returned and what the page helped them accomplish.

A small audience experiment

Choose one article, identify the reader it serves and share a faithful explanation in an appropriate place. Record the questions and responses. Improve the article where the evidence warrants it, then offer a relevant next read. A useful learning cycle can begin before a large audience exists.

Article 12 / Commercial direction

Earning from Nigeria by Creating Value for the World

No international earnings, paid customers or active affiliate partnership are claimed.

I want an international presence because useful work should have the opportunity to travel. Knowledge, judgment or a practical skill may address a need beyond my immediate environment.

The challenge is making that value visible, understandable and purchasable.

I am exploring the commercial possibility. This is a direction I want to pursue, rather than a claim that international income has already followed.

Begin with a recognised problem

My interests provide subjects. A buyer needs a reason to spend money.

What are they trying to accomplish? Where are they struggling? What would a useful result look like?

An article can investigate the question. A practical resource can demonstrate an approach. A conversation with a potential buyer can reveal whether the difficulty is important enough to justify payment.

I want an offer to emerge from that understanding.

Make the deliverable clear

“Insight” is difficult to assess before purchase. A defined research brief, workshop or resource gives someone a more concrete basis for consideration.

The description should explain what they receive, what information it depends on and what requires their participation. Outcomes outside my control need clear limits.

My legal background makes me attentive to those boundaries. They help both sides understand the engagement.

Demonstrate capability publicly

A website allows someone to examine my thinking before making an enquiry. An essay can show care with evidence. A project can show how I approach a problem. A worksheet can let someone try part of a method.

These examples do not guarantee a sale. They give a buyer a basis for considering one.

My phrase “global-rate value” means work that can justify a fair price in a wider market. Visibility alone does not establish that price. Scope, costs, expertise, alternatives and dependable delivery all matter.

Treat referrals as a specific opportunity

Some tools may offer affiliate programmes. Where an approved relationship exists, a useful account could include a clearly disclosed referral link.

The experience must support the recommendation. I need to explain its circumstances and limitations, and check the current programme terms before treating it as an opportunity.

This collection currently contains no affiliate purchase links and does not announce a sponsored partnership. Any later relationship should be disclosed beside the relevant recommendation.

Deliver what was agreed

Attracting a buyer creates an obligation. Deadlines, revisions and support need clear expectations. The offer should fit what I can realistically provide.

The transaction also needs an understandable route: identity, scope, price, payment and what happens afterwards. The particular arrangements and obligations require checking for the actual engagement.

An initial delivery can teach me whether the offer was clear, useful and sustainable to repeat.

A first offer brief

Write down the buyer, problem, deliverable, scope limits, delivery time, required inputs and full cost to fulfil. Then discuss the problem with a potential buyer before building a large commercial system around it.

Article 13 / Closing reflection

Learning to Use AI Well: A Nigerian’s Practical Account

My interest in AI has become practical. Can it help me publish clearly, develop a website or work through an unfamiliar problem?

My attempts have brought encouragement and frustration. Ideas have become visible projects. Misunderstandings, broken references and unfinished investigations have also appeared.

Those experiences are changing how I judge the process. I want to know what I can understand, verify and use afterwards.

Start with knowledge you possess

As a lawyer, I bring questions about claims and evidence. What is established? What remains uncertain? Does the conclusion go beyond the supporting facts?

Someone experienced in teaching, farming or retail will recognise things I may miss. Their knowledge provides a basis for directing and assessing assistance.

For a person wondering where to begin, a familiar task offers something against which to judge the answer.

Explain circumstances and correct drift

When developing NaijaOS, the existing design and content mattered. A proposal needed to respect that work. My device, audience and publishing arrangement can also change what help is relevant.

I need to provide the details that affect the task.

Then I need to read carefully. A fluent response may introduce an assumption or change the emphasis. Correcting it early prevents an inaccurate interpretation from becoming the foundation of later work.

Turn explanations into understanding

I can ask for a concept to be explained through my own project. Afterwards, I can attempt a small task or explain the idea in my words.

That checks whether the explanation became usable knowledge.

Troubleshooting also requires separating an observation from a possible cause. An error is evidence; an explanation may still need testing. A suggested change needs its result checked.

That discipline prevents confidence from outrunning the record.

Take care with what leaves the conversation

Private exploration and public work have different consequences. Before publishing, I need to examine claims and references. Before releasing a tool, I need appropriate evidence about its behaviour.

I also need judgment about what enters the conversation. Professional work can contain information that should not be casually copied into an external service.

Using AI well includes deciding what to share, check and publish.

Describe the Nigerian context precisely

Nigerians differ in devices, connectivity, resources and goals. My experience as a lawyer building websites cannot represent everyone.

The useful account identifies my circumstances, attempts and remaining difficulties. A reader can then assess what applies to them.

I want the results to become durable: reviewed articles, organised notes, maintained projects and documented decisions. Otherwise, an early suggestion can later be confused with the approach actually adopted.

Measure a change in capability

Did I understand more clearly, complete a task accurately or become able to do something with less assistance? Those questions matter more to me than the volume produced.

AI has widened what I am willing to attempt. Learning, verification and follow-through determine what those attempts become.

Your next useful session

Choose a familiar task and define the intended result. Provide relevant context without unnecessary private information. Make an attempt, inspect the outcome and preserve what you learned. Finish by identifying what you can now do and what still requires help.

Your First Project Brief

Companion to How I Build from Nigeria, by Paul A. Magaji.

Use this on paper or copy it into a document. This page does not collect or save answers.

Purpose

Who is the project for? What difficulty are they trying to address? What should they be able to do after using it?

The smallest useful version

What is the single task you will support first? What will you deliberately leave for later? Which existing work must be preserved?

Resources and costs

Which skills, tools, time and assistance are needed? Record initial and continuing costs separately. Date any prices or terms you check.

Evidence and responsibility

Which claims need sources? What information must remain private? Who might rely on the result, and what limitations should be visible?

The first test

Describe the exact task you will attempt. What observable result would count as success? What would make you revise or stop?

Change record

Record the date, starting state, reason for the change, intervention and observed result. Keep unresolved questions separate from confirmed findings.

Publication and maintenance

Which version is being reviewed? Check the links, main tasks, mobile use and any downloads. What remains unverified? Who will correct problems, and how can the change be withdrawn if necessary?

Reflection

What can you now do or explain? What still requires help? What is the next useful step?
