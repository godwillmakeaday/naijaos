Method

From public signal to a decision worth reviewing.

NaijaOS provides decision intelligence, not automatic instructions. Businesses verify internal costs and retain responsibility for every final decision.

01 / Observe

Gather dated evidence

Official releases, public records, reported indicators and verified operating observations.

02 / Connect

Trace transmission

Examine how change moves across fuel, FX, transport, supplier cost and inflation.

03 / Interpret

Identify exposure

Locate likely effects on inventory, delivery costs, replacement prices, credit and margins.

04 / Review

Record the decision

Structure a signal, record whether the business reordered, repriced or waited, then review the outcome.

GEOGRAPHY / ABUJA–LAFIA

Route costs may reach margins before FX does.

Transport pressure increased in this illustration while FX conditions remained relatively stable. Businesses replacing locally manufactured stock may face greater immediate exposure from route and delivery costs than from currency movement.

Decision to review: Repricing.
Before applying a general increase, review Abuja–Lafia collection costs, minimum profitable delivery orders and margins on products replenished within the next 14 days.

Verification required: current supplier quotation and actual route cost.

Distributor Operating Brief

Join the first NaijaOS operating-intelligence pilot.

We are working with a small number of qualifying distributors to understand how changing operating conditions affect weekly purchasing, pricing and distribution decisions.

Who fits

Packaged-food or household-essential distributors with two or more locations, regular replenishment and meaningful route costs.

What participants receive

An exposure profile, weekly Operating Brief, reorder/reprice/wait signals and a decision-review record.

What we request

Routes, product categories, replenishment rhythm, approximate cost structure and feedback—not confidential records.
