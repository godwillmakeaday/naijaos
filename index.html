§ 05 — Source discipline

Diagnosis, separated from recommendation

naijaOS works from public information, official releases, reported indicators, and editorial interpretation. We read public signals for practical understanding; we do not claim ownership over public information, and we keep the difference between what the environment is doing and what you might do about it visible on every page.

This is intelligence, not advice. naijaOS is not legal, financial, tax, or investment advice. Figures and conditions change. Verify against current official sources before acting. The platform separates diagnosis from recommendation, and welcomes corrections and updates.
