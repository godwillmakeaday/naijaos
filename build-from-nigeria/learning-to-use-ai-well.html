Learning to Use AI Well: A Nigerian’s Practical Account

Context, evidence and growing capability in a Nigerian builder’s account.

By Paul A. Magaji · 3 min read · Closing reflection

In this article

  2. Explain circumstances and correct drift
  3. Turn explanations into understanding
  4. Take care with what leaves the conversation
  5. Describe the Nigerian context precisely
  6. Measure a change in capability
  7. Your next useful session

My interest in AI has become practical. Can it help me publish clearly, develop a website or work through an unfamiliar problem?

My attempts have brought encouragement and frustration. Ideas have become visible projects. Misunderstandings, broken references and unfinished investigations have also appeared.

Those experiences are changing how I judge the process. I want to know what I can understand, verify and use afterwards.

Start with knowledge you possess

As a lawyer, I bring questions about claims and evidence. What is established? What remains uncertain? Does the conclusion go beyond the supporting facts?

Someone experienced in teaching, farming or retail will recognise things I may miss. Their knowledge provides a basis for directing and assessing assistance.

For a person wondering where to begin, a familiar task offers something against which to judge the answer.

Explain circumstances and correct drift

When developing NaijaOS, the existing design and content mattered. A proposal needed to respect that work. My device, audience and publishing arrangement can also change what help is relevant.

I need to provide the details that affect the task.

Then I need to read carefully. A fluent response may introduce an assumption or change the emphasis. Correcting it early prevents an inaccurate interpretation from becoming the foundation of later work.

Turn explanations into understanding

I can ask for a concept to be explained through my own project. Afterwards, I can attempt a small task or explain the idea in my words.

That checks whether the explanation became usable knowledge.

Troubleshooting also requires separating an observation from a possible cause. An error is evidence; an explanation may still need testing. A suggested change needs its result checked.

That discipline prevents confidence from outrunning the record.

Take care with what leaves the conversation

Private exploration and public work have different consequences. Before publishing, I need to examine claims and references. Before releasing a tool, I need appropriate evidence about its behaviour.

I also need judgment about what enters the conversation. Professional work can contain information that should not be casually copied into an external service.

Using AI well includes deciding what to share, check and publish.

Describe the Nigerian context precisely

Nigerians differ in devices, connectivity, resources and goals. My experience as a lawyer building websites cannot represent everyone.

The useful account identifies my circumstances, attempts and remaining difficulties. A reader can then assess what applies to them.

I want the results to become durable: reviewed articles, organised notes, maintained projects and documented decisions. Otherwise, an early suggestion can later be confused with the approach actually adopted.

Measure a change in capability

Did I understand more clearly, complete a task accurately or become able to do something with less assistance? Those questions matter more to me than the volume produced.

AI has widened what I am willing to attempt. Learning, verification and follow-through determine what those attempts become.

Your next useful session

Choose a familiar task and define the intended result. Provide relevant context without unnecessary private information. Make an attempt, inspect the outcome and preserve what you learned. Finish by identifying what you can now do and what still requires help.

Keep the work
