First commercial product

The NaijaOS Distributor Operating Brief.

A weekly operating-intelligence brief helping distributors decide whether to reorder, reprice or wait during the coming seven days.

Apply for the pilotView sample structure
Designed for

Businesses exposed to repeated replacement decisions.

The first pilot focuses on packaged-food and household-essential distributors operating across two or more locations, replenishing frequently and carrying meaningful collection or delivery costs.

CustomerMulti-location distributors
Decision cycleWeekly
Decision statesReorder · Reprice · Wait
Exposure model

Five variables, read together.

The Brief connects public conditions to the business’s routes, products, replenishment rhythm and margin exposure.

Fuel + transport

Collection, haulage, last-mile delivery, fleet cost and minimum profitable order.

FX + essential inputs

Imported exposure, replacement prices, supplier quotations, availability and product mix.

Inflation + demand

Pricing pressure, purchasing power, stock velocity, customer credit and margin.

Weekly output

A structured brief, not a prediction machine.

Each issue separates observation, interpretation, company verification and the final decision.

1. Conditions this week

Dated direction of the five variables, with source and geographic scope.

2. Transmission

What changed, what caused it, what it may affect and how soon the effect may arrive.

3. Company exposure

Routes, product categories, replacement windows, delivery cost, credit and margin areas requiring review.

4. Decision signal

Reorder, reprice or wait—with confidence level and items requiring human verification.

5. Decision record

What the business decided, why, and what happened when the outcome was later reviewed.

Limitation: NaijaOS provides decision intelligence, not automatic instructions. Participants verify supplier quotations, actual route costs and internal commercial information before acting.
